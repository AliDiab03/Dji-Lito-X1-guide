Privacy Policy

Dji Lito X1 Guide · Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Dji Lito X1 Guide (the “App”) handles information. The App is a free, unofficial guide for drone users. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by DJI. All product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

The App does not require an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal details. We do not run our own servers that store user data.

The App stores the following on your device only, and it never leaves your device:

  • Your progress in guides, such as ticked checklist items and maintenance tasks.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads (banner, interstitial, and app-open ads) provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID (for example, the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • App interaction and ad performance data (impressions, clicks, crashes and diagnostics)

We do not receive your personal data from these services. Google’s use of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your ad choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

4. Internet connection

The App needs an internet connection to work. It checks whether your device is online, and it loads ads and web pages over the internet. The App does not read your browsing history or your contacts, and it does not access your camera, microphone, photos, or precise location.

5. Third-party websites

Some screens open websites inside the App, for example the official DJI website, support, and store pages. These sites are operated by third parties and have their own privacy policies and cookies. We are not responsible for their content or practices. Please review their policies before sharing any information with them.

6. Product manual (PDF)

The product manual is bundled inside the App and is opened on your device. Viewing it does not send any information anywhere.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

8. Data security and retention

We do not collect or store your personal data on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to retain or share. Data saved on your device is removed when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled under Google’s policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, or objection to processing. Because we do not hold your personal data, these requests mostly apply to the ad data handled by Google. You can manage it through your Google account privacy settings and your device’s ad settings.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the new policy.
